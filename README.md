# Blackjack Table

A 2D blackjack game in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There's no build step: open `index.html` in a browser to play.

You sit in the middle seat with four NPCs at the table. Each one plays and bets in their own way and reacts to what happens:

| NPC | Style |
| --- | --- |
| **Lucky Lou** (third base) | Plays perfect basic strategy. Comments when you make a play that isn't by the book, and the **Ask Lou** button gives you his advice. |
| **Vera** | Plays cautiously and stands on any hard 12+. Complains when someone "takes the dealer's bust card". |
| **Big Tony** | High roller. Hits until 18, doubles a lot, and sometimes splits tens. |
| **Mia** | Nervous beginner. Mostly follows the strategy chart, but sometimes guesses. |

Rules: 6-deck shoe reshuffled at 75% penetration, dealer stands on all 17s, blackjack pays 3:2, you can double on any first two cards (including after a split), split up to 4 hands, and split aces get one card each.

Controls: click chips to bet, then **Deal** (or press Enter). During your turn, press **H**it, **S**tand, **D**ouble or s**P**lit.
